Trials that tend to end in longer are those designed around oncology populations with measurable, prospectively documented disease burden and prior treatment history, where functional capacity, growth factor/restriction criteria, and tumor response/progression endpoints define a more advanced or chronic disease trajectory. Trials that tend to end in shorter are those with broader or safety-filtered enrollment, such as COVID-19 interventions, healthy volunteer plus patient designs, ECG/imaging safety exclusions, investigator-discretion exclusions, numeric lab/score thresholds, dermatologic restrictions, or height-based derived variables.
